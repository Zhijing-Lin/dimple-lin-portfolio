Interactive Coaching Through Feedback
An interactive Storyline branching scenario that helps new managers practice giving clear, respectful, and actionable feedback during a performance conversation.

Try out the course
Problem
New managers often struggle to balance honesty, clarity, empathy, and actionability during performance conversations. Poor responses can reduce trust, create defensiveness, or leave employees unsure of what to do next.

This module provides a low-risk environment to practice those decisions before applying them in the workplace.

Solution
I built a Storyline branching scenario that simulates a realistic manager–employee feedback conversation.

Learners make decisions at key moments, receive immediate, consequence-based feedback, and see how their responses affect trust, clarity, and the direction of the conversation.

Rather than presenting feedback as a list of best practices, the module turns it into applied decision-making practice.


Design Approach
I identified the key decisions managers make during a feedback conversation and translated them into a realistic branching scenario.

Each decision point reflects a common managerial challenge, such as:

Being too vague

Avoiding difficult feedback

Responding defensively

Failing to provide actionable next steps

Learners practice choosing responses and seeing the consequences of each approach.

Branching Scenario Flow Map
Before developing the module in Storyline, I mapped the employee dialogue, manager choices, feedback, recovery paths, and final outcomes to ensure each branch reflected a meaningful workplace behavior.

Learners move through the scenario by choosing how to respond during a feedback conversation. Effective choices move the conversation toward greater trust and clarity, while ineffective choices trigger consequence-based feedback explaining why the response may create confusion, defensiveness, or reduce trust. Some branches also allow learners to recover from mistakes, reflecting how real feedback conversations can be repaired.

Flow Map Legend

Black rectangle — Employee dialogue

Orange diamond — Manager decision

Green rectangle — Effective response / feedback

Pink rectangle — Ineffective response / feedback

Red circle — Final outcome

Next Steps & Reflection
In the next iteration, I would test the scenario with target learners to identify where they hesitate, which mistakes occur most often, and where additional scaffolding is needed.

This project strengthened my ability to translate a complex interpersonal skill into structured, scenario-based decision practice and connect each learner choice to a realistic workplace consequence.
