Adaptive Practice and AI-Powered Feedback for Corporate Training
An adaptive, AI-powered corporate training module that helps managers recognize, revise, and write effective performance feedback through scenario-based assessment, remediation, and targeted open-response feedback.

Problem
Managers are often expected to provide written performance feedback with limited training on how to make it specific, actionable, and development-oriented.

Traditional training frequently relies on multiple-choice questions or generic examples, giving learners few opportunities to practice writing feedback in their own words and receive targeted guidance.

The key learning gap was therefore:

How can learners move from recognizing good feedback to independently writing it?

Solution
I designed an adaptive Captivate learning experience that progresses from analysis to independent writing.

Learners:

Analyze realistic workplace feedback

Complete a scenario-based assessment

Enter targeted remediation when additional support is needed

Practice revising feedback in their own words

Receive AI-powered, criterion-based feedback

This structure provides additional support when learners struggle while still moving stronger learners directly into application.

Adaptive Practice & Remediation
This project combines adaptive practice and AI-supported feedback to help learners move from recognizing effective feedback to writing it independently.

Learners first analyze realistic workplace examples and identify common issues, such as vague language, missing impact, unclear next steps, and judgment-based tone. They then complete a scenario-based assessment. I used Captivate variables and advanced actions to track performance and trigger a remediation pathway when additional support is needed. After remediation, learners retry the assessment before completing an open-ended writing task with API-powered targeted feedback.

The module includes:

Scenario-based feedback analysis

Mastery tracking

Performance-triggered remediation

Assessment retry

Open-ended writing practice

AI-powered targeted feedback

AI-Powered Targeted Feedback
The images below illustrate the flow of the AI-powered feedback system embedded in the course. Learners submit an open-ended response and immediately receive targeted guidance based on the quality of their writing. The AI feedback evaluates whether the response includes specific observable behavior, clear impact, actionable next steps, and professional tone. It also shows what the learner did well, what criteria are missing, and how they can improve their next response.
